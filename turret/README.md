# Pan-tilt turret (v2)

Pan-tilt mount for a laser now and a Nerf Rival Perses later, driven by two Feetech STS3215 12 V servos on a
Waveshare Bus Servo Adapter (A), with an Arduino Nano switching the laser. Everything electronic rides on the
turntable, so both servos plug into the board with their **stock 152 mm leads** and only power + one USB cable
cross the pan joint.

- **Pan (v2: flipped servo):** the pan servo hangs upside down in a sleeve under the turntable, so its body turns
  with the turret. Its horn points down onto a fixed post on the base floor. The floor cap sits on top, inside
  the electronics box, so the pan lead comes straight up into the box. The turret's weight rides on 3x 6802 rollers
  on the deck ring. Pan software limit +-120 deg (service loop). **Pan direction is reversed in software.**
- **Tilt (unchanged):** servo in the right tower sleeve, 6802 idler in the left tower, payload hinged at z 215.
  Laser plate +-40 deg, Perses cradle +-20 deg.
- **Electronics box** between the towers (101 x 88 x 75 mm, interior 96 x 83, 57 mm above the tray). The servo
  board is zip-tied high on the -y wall with its servo ports up. The Nano slides into rails on the tray, and a
  small USB hub sits where a Raspberry Pi 5 goes later (the tray has the Pi's 58 x 49 M2.5 holes, and the box
  has room for a Pi 5 with Active Cooler). The box shape avoids the Perses grip and trigger guard, which swing
  down between the towers on the -x side.
- **Cable channel:** a walled ring on the deck (r 79-99) under the turntable rim. The 12 V and USB service loop
  lies there, walled off from the rollers.

Everything is generated by `perses_turret.py` (parameters at the top). `fit_check.py` re-runs every check
(`TURRET_PAYLOAD=perses` for the cradle). `export_assembly.py` writes the multi-body assembly
(`turret_assembly.3mf` / `.glb`: every part is its own named body, bought hardware prefixed `HW_`).
The v1 design (servo board under the deck) is kept as `perses_turret_v1.py`.

## Hardware
- 2x STS3215 12 V, Waveshare Bus Servo Adapter (A) (42 x 33 mm), 12 V 5 A supply
- Arduino Nano (old one is fine), 2N2222 NPN, 1 kOhm resistor, small perfboard, 6 mm mini toggle switch (laser ARM)
- Small USB 2.0 hub, 4 ports (board, Nano, webcam). The Pi 5 replaces it later.
- 4x 6802-2RS (3 rollers + 1 tilt idler)
- M3 socket head: 10x M3x6, 11x M3x8, 8x M3x10, 11x M3x12, **4x M3x20** (box + tray to the turntable; check your kit)
- 36x M3 heat-set inserts, length 5 (hole d4.0 x 6 deep)
- Zip ties (2.5 mm): servo board, hub, perfboard, two strain reliefs
- Optional: 0.5 mm M3 shim washers between the post and the pan horn if the rollers lift off the deck

| From | Path | To |

| Laser red | lid hole, then the ARM toggle (+x wall) | Nano 5V |

| Webcam | lid hole (-x side) | USB hub |

Cable reach (fit_check section 6, with 15% added for bends): pan lead 107 mm, tilt lead 131 mm, both under the
stock 152 mm. Servo IDs: pan 7, tilt 8. Centre both horns (position 2048) before mounting.

## Assembly order
1. Heat-set inserts: 4 in the turntable's servo sleeve rim (top face), 8 tower + 4 box in the turntable top,
   3 in the roller posts (horizontal), 4 in the base_frame skirt bosses (from below), 4 in the box's top corner
   bosses, 4 in the tower_servo sleeve, 3 in the cradle's left ear, 1 in the stub shaft end, 1 under the laser bore.
2. Base: base_frame onto base_floor, 4x M3x6 from under the floor. Cable tray onto the deck (3 pegs, a dab of glue).
3. Rollers: bearing on each roller axle, M3x12 through the axle into the turntable post.
4. Pan servo (centred): drop it into the turntable sleeve from the top, horn down through the window. Pan floor cap
   on top, lead out of the slot, 4x M3x12.
5. Set the turntable on the base: horn on the post, rollers on the deck. From under the base, 4x M3x6 up through
   the post into the horn (long hex key through the floor).
6. Towers on the turntable, 4x M3x10 each. Press the 6802 into the left tower.
7. Tilt servo into the right tower sleeve, floor cap 4x M3x12 (same as v1).
8. Box: zip-tie the board high on the -y wall (servo ports up). Set the box on the turntable, drop the tray on its
   corner seats, 4x M3x20 through the tray and box into the turntable. Nano into the rails, hub and perfboard
   zip-tied, wire up, lid on with 4x M3x8.
9. Payload: stub shaft, horn spacer and ear screws as in v1 (7 screws). Laser, webcam.

## Fit check (fit_report.txt, fit_report_perses.txt)
No collisions at rest, through the full tilt sweep (laser +-40, Perses +-20), or through 360 deg of pan, for both
payloads. Over the box, the lid clears the laser plate by 29.7 mm at 40 deg tilt and the Perses grip and trigger guard
by 6.8 mm at 20 deg (the Perses proxy is built from photos, so measure the real grip before printing the cradle). Hanging sleeve sweep r46.8 in the r50 deck hole. Post neck r13 in the r13.5 horn window. Rollers on the
deck with 1 mm under the turntable. Every screw: head clear, shank clear, 4.4-5.0 mm in inserts, 2.4 mm in the
horn threads. Both stock servo leads reach the board along cable paths checked to be clear of every part, with the
Pi 5 in place too.
