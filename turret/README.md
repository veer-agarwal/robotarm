# Perses pan-tilt turret

Pan-tilt mount for a Nerf Rival Perses (upright: its hopper is gravity fed, so it can't lie on its side),
driven by two Feetech STS3215 12 V servos on their own bus board and 12 V supply.

- Pan: servo upright in the base, turntable bolted to the horn, weight carried by 3x 6802 rollers on the deck.
- Tilt: servo in a sleeve on the right tower, 6802 idler in the left tower, cradle hinged under the blaster's
  centre of mass. Software limit +-20 deg (hopper feed; also the grip-to-turntable clearance).
- Holding torque at 20 deg tilt ~5.6 kg-cm vs 30 kg-cm stall. Overall ~200 x 200 x 435 mm with the Perses.

Everything is generated by `perses_turret.py` (parameters at the top); `fit_check.py` re-runs every check.
Servo geometry comes from TheRobotStudio's official `sts3215_03a_v1.stl`.

## Payload: laser plate now, Perses cradle later
`laser_plate.stl` bolts to the same hinge as the cradle (same ears, horn spacer, stub shaft and screws), so
swapping later is 7 screws. It carries the VLM-650-01 laser (10.5 mm bore, M3x6 set screw into an insert from
the top) at the front and the webcam on a 20 mm riser behind it (1/4-20 screw from below, or two velcro slots
for a clip webcam), so the lens sees over the laser. With the light payload the tilt limit is +-40 deg.
Print it upside down (block and riser tops on the bed), 30% infill, no supports; 120 x 90 mm fits either printer.
`TURRET_PAYLOAD=perses python fit_check.py` re-checks the cradle configuration.

## Measure first
The Perses has no published body dimensions; the cradle is sized from the box (25 x 6 x 13 in) and photos.
Before printing the cradle, measure the blaster and edit these in `perses_turret.py`, then rerun:
`CRADLE_IN` (body width + ~5 mm for foam), the floor window `(-135..25, +-27)` for the grip and battery
guard, and `P_L/P_W/P_H` if you want the fit check to be exact. Put the hinge (x = 0) under the loaded
blaster's balance point.

## Hardware
- 2x STS3215 12 V, bus board, 12 V 5 A supply (ordered)
- 4x 6802-2RS (3 rollers + 1 tilt idler; you have 10)
- M3 socket head (KADRICK kit): 5x M3x6, 8x M3x8, 8x M3x10, 11x M3x12
- 24x M3 heat-set inserts, length 5 (hole d4.0 x 6 deep)
- 2x 25 mm Velcro straps (blaster), optional 4x M4 screws to fix the base to a board
- Laser: VLM-650-01 (10.5 mm body) held by an M3x8 set screw from below; webcam on a 1/4-20 screw or its clip

## Assembly order
1. Heat-set all inserts: 4 in each servo sleeve's open end, 8 in the turntable top (tower feet),
   3 in the turntable roller posts (horizontal), 3 in the cradle's left ear, 1 in the stub shaft end, 1 under the laser bore.
2. Rollers: bearing on each roller axle, M3x12 through the axle into the turntable post.
3. Pan servo: set the horn to centre (ID 7, position 2048) before mounting. Slide it into the base sleeve from
   below, horn up through the deck window, lead out of the floor cap slot, floor cap on with 4x M3x12.
4. Turntable on the pan horn, 4x M3x6 through the counterbored hub (long hex key). Rollers rest on the deck.
5. Towers on the turntable, 4x M3x10 each. Press the 6802 into the left tower from outside.
6. Tilt servo (ID 8, centred) into the right tower sleeve from outside, horn through the window, floor cap 4x M3x12.
   Lead down through the turntable hole.
7. Stub shaft through the cradle's left ear, 3x M3x8 from inside. Slide the stub into the left bearing,
   horn spacer between the tilt horn and the right ear, 4x M3x8 from inside the cradle through the ear.
   Washer + M3x6 on the stub end.
8. Blaster in, two straps through the wall slots, laser in its bore, webcam on the bracket.

## Fit check (fit_report.txt)
No collisions at rest, through the full +-20 deg tilt sweep, or through 360 deg of pan. Rollers sit on the deck
with 1 mm under the turntable. 1.7 mm between each ear and its tower. Every screw: head clear, shank clear,
engagement 4.4-4.9 mm in inserts and 2.4 mm in the 2.6 mm deep horn threads, tips 1.3+ mm above the servo body.
